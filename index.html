How to Play

Controls:

  • WASD / Arrow Keys - Move your manager around the office
  • C Key - Dump the coffee (disables coffee breaks for 1 minute)
  • B Key - Close bathroom for cleaning (disables bathroom for 1 minute)
  • Space - Send nearby employee back to desk when colliding

Objective:

Reach the revenue target before the workday ends without any employee going insane!

Tips:

  • Walking near employees increases their productivity but also their insanity
  • Blocking breaks increases revenue but watch employee sanity levels
  • Balance productivity and sanity - if any employee hits 100 insanity, it's game over!
